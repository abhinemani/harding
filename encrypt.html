Encrypt a team key

This page runs entirely in your browser and sends nothing anywhere. Paste a dedicated Anthropic API key and choose a passphrase; you get back an encrypted string to commit as team-key.json. Only people who know the passphrase can unlock the key.

Passphrases are matched ignoring case and extra spaces. Derivation: PBKDF2-SHA256, 300,000 rounds, 16-byte salt → AES-256-GCM with a 12-byte nonce. Rotating the key later is the same three steps with a new key.
